    What is Padhai Buddy?
    A study buddy that turns your own notes into exam-style practice questions, asks them one at a time in a chat, and brings back the ones you get wrong until you've learned them.
    How does it turn my notes into questions?
    1. Paste your notes or upload a PDF.
    2. Pick an exam style, a language and how many questions you want.
    3. Press Generate quiz. Claude writes questions using only what's in your notes.
    4. Answer in the chat. Each answer shows the sentence from your notes that proves it.
    Can I upload a PDF?
    Yes. Use Upload PDF or drop the file onto the notes box. The text is pulled out in your browser and shown in the box, so you can check or trim it before generating. Scanned PDFs (photos of pages) have no selectable text, so paste those notes instead.
    How does revision work?
    Every question you miss joins your revision queue. It comes back 1 day later, then after 3 days, then after 7 days. Get it right at each stage and it's marked mastered. Miss it again and it starts over from 1 day.
    How do I answer?
    Tap an option, or type A, B, C or D in the message box. When questions are due, typing START begins your revision.
    Where are my notes saved?
    Only in this browser, on this device. Your notes, revision queue and progress are kept in the browser's own storage. There's no account and no copy on a server.
    Does my PDF get uploaded?
    No. The PDF is opened and read inside your browser. Only the text you see in the notes box is kept, and the file itself never leaves your device.
    What is sent when I press Generate?
    Just the note text, once, to Claude so it can write your questions. Nothing is sent while you type, upload a PDF or answer questions.
    Do I need an account?
    No. Open the page and start. Because there's no account, your progress doesn't sync between devices.
    How do I delete everything?
    Use Clear my data on this page, or clear your browser's site data. Either removes everything Padhai Buddy saved on this device.
    Which exams does it support?
    • NEET: one-correct MCQs, assertion–reason and match the column.
    • JEE Main: conceptual MCQs, plus numericals when your notes have numbers.
    • UPSC Prelims: statement-based questions.
    • CBSE Boards: competency-based MCQs and assertion–reason.
    • Simple MCQ: quick revision for anything else.
    Can I get questions in Hindi?
    Yes. Choose हिन्दी for Hindi in Devanagari, or Hinglish for Hindi in Roman script mixed with English terms. Your notes can be in either language.
    How do I know the answers are right?
    Questions are written only from your notes, and every answer shows the line from your notes that backs it up. A ✓ means that line matches your notes word for word.
